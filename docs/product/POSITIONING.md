# POSITIONING
## AI Creative Workstation

---

## Positioning Statement

**For** skilled creators — content producers, solopreneurs, indie developers, and small studios — **who** are blocked by the complexity of professional AI tools or the creative ceiling of simplified cloud tools, **Canvas** (working title) **is** a local-first AI creative workstation **that** makes the entire spectrum of AI creative production — from single images to complete video productions — feel like natural creative work.

**Unlike** ComfyUI and similar tools that expose technical complexity, **or** cloud services like Midjourney and Runway that have no project memory, local option, or cross-modal continuity, **Canvas** maintains persistent creative assets — characters, styles, environments — that travel coherently from image to 3D to video, all running on the creator's own hardware.

---

## Market Category

**We are creating a new category:** The AI Creative OS.

Not "AI image generator with extra features."  
Not "ComfyUI with a better UI."  
Not "creative suite with AI plugins."

**The AI Creative OS is:**
- The environment where AI creative production happens
- A system that remembers your creative entities across sessions and modalities
- A hardware-aware orchestrator that routes work intelligently between local and cloud
- A creative workstation where models are infrastructure, not choices

This is similar to how Adobe didn't just make a "better paint program" — they created the category of professional digital creative tools. We are creating the category of AI-native creative production.

## Unique Value Proposition

### Headline
**Your creative ideas. Your hardware. No configuration.**

### Sub-headline
**Create images, 3D assets, videos, and complete content packages — without learning a single AI concept. Your characters, styles, and assets persist across every creation, across every modality.**

### The Three Promises

**1. Create, Don't Configure**
Photo → Intent → Result. The application handles every technical decision.

**2. Your Work Stays on Your Machine**
By default, your photos, client work, and creative library never leave your computer. Cloud is always your choice, never the default.

**3. Build Once, Use Everywhere**
Define a character, a style, or a product once. Use it in images, 3D models, videos, and content packages — consistently, forever.

## Pricing Philosophy

**The goal is to make local inference feel like owning a professional tool, not renting a service.**

| Tier | Model | Target User |
|------|-------|------------|
| **Free** | Local generation only; limited to curated models | Yuki (aspiring creator) — discovery and growth |
| **Creator** | Local + cloud credits; full model library | Marcus, Priya — professional creators |
| **Studio** | Team assets; higher cloud quota; shared model registry | Priya's clients; small studios |
| **Enterprise** | Private cloud; team collaboration; API access | Studios, agencies, media companies |

**Critical pricing principle:** Cloud credits should be a luxury, not a requirement. A user with capable hardware should be able to use the full product without ever buying cloud credits.

## Go-to-Market Approach

### Launch Strategy: Virality Through Output Quality

The most powerful marketing is a creator who publishes YouTube content that was made with Canvas and says "I made this on my Mac, locally, in 20 minutes." Visual quality and workflow speed are the demo.

### Target Communities at Launch
1. YouTube creator community (Marcus persona)
2. Indie game developer communities (Sam persona)
3. Apple Silicon enthusiast communities (local inference interest is already high)
4. Solopreneur / creator economy communities

### Content Strategy
- "Made with Canvas" examples — not abstract demos, real content
- "Previously 5 tools, now 1" narrative
- Privacy/local-first messaging for professional creators
- Comparison videos: "ComfyUI setup vs Canvas setup for the same workflow"

### Distribution
- Mac App Store (trust signal; discoverability)
- Direct download (no AppStore restrictions for advanced features)
- Discord community for early adopters
- Product Hunt launch
